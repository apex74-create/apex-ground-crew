Sample Bakery

Fresh every morning — Johannesburg, Michigan.

We bake breads, rolls, and sweet rolls fresh every morning in Johannesburg, Michigan. When they're gone, they're gone — come early or order ahead.

What we bake

  • Breads — white, wheat, and rye loaves, baked at sunrise.
  • Rolls — dinner rolls and sandwich rolls, by the half dozen or dozen.
  • Sweet rolls — cinnamon and sticky rolls, the morning favorite.

How to order

Walk in any morning and pick from the case — that's the easy way.

For a dozen or more, order the day before so your box is ready when you arrive. Tell us what you want, how many, and the morning you're coming. We'll have it boxed and waiting.

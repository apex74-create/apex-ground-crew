# Apex Ground Crew

**You fly the business, we work the ground.**

Apex Ground Crew is the last-mile ground crew for small businesses. You tell us about your business in plain words; the crew does the ground work — a one-page website that says what you do, business listings set up straight, simple forms and packets assembled from your words, small jobs finished end-to-end and handed back.

One business, one crew, one job at a time — done right, brought back, and yours.

**Live page:** https://apex74-create.github.io/apex-ground-crew/

## How it starts

Fill in the form on the live page. A real person-and-machine crew reads it, files your job, and comes back with the first step. No account. No jargon. Your words stay yours.

## This repo

- `index.html` — the one-page site + intake form (GitHub Pages).
- `demo/` — the v0 demonstration run: the sample scope filed through the form, the deliverable produced from it, and the run log. The door, connected to the machine — proven, not posted.

What you're getting

Ten fishing watch games + the dashboard. One engine, ten waters: largemouth bass, Michigan steelhead, rainbow trout, walleye, northern pike, muskie, Chinook salmon, bluegill, smallmouth bass, blue marlin — each paired with its classic lure. Cast from the wrist, fight the fish with a finger on the line; a landed fish mounts on the wall as a clock — new fish, new size and colors, every catch.

The leaderboard. Every catch posts to the board: species records, placings, records that stand until broken. Three placed fish of one species earn the Triangle — a three-head mount that tells the time and date. The board works inside the dashboard today; the shared community board is on the clock below.

His storefront. A store in his name selling applications — 24 picked by category from a shelf of working software, the fishing line first — with fresh stock dropping on a regular cadence and half the shelf held back so drops never run dry.

The road. The same engine swaps one word and becomes the next sport: rifleman sports and archery sports as named lines of their own, then bird, then cloven-hoof game, then automobiles (bracket racing in the browser). That is the road ahead — stated as the road, not as goods in hand today.
